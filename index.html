About Me

I am a PhD student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at Tel Aviv University. My research interests include machine learning, optimization, and learning theory.

I obtained my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Computer Science from The Open University of Israel, and an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the Tel Aviv University, where I worked under the supervision of Prof.Roi Livni.
